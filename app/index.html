Built from the official descriptions + cited launch guides. One trophy is missable outright; two more are time-sensitive — their windows close mid-story.

No mission replay, no New Game+

There is no way to replay a story mission and no New Game+. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter (plus one before the final mission). The game offers 15 manual slots plus autosave — use them. A chapter-start save is the only way back if you miss something.

Sources: PowerPyx, OutputLag, KeenGamer, eGamers — retrieved 2026-11-20.

Cheats disable trophies and saving

Cheats turn off trophies and saving on that save. The game has dozens of cheats, but they only unlock after the credits — and they must never be used on your trophy save. Keep one clean save for the whole platinum run.

Sources: PowerPyx, OutputLag — retrieved 2026-11-20.

Sources & honesty

Every guide cites at least one source; every step cites at least one source. Nothing here copies another guide's text — the guides are written in our own words from the official list and the sources below.

SOURCES DISAGREE — both versions shown

Version A — PowerPyx, KeenGamer: 18 meditation spots; the early Chapter 1 spot is at Jason's house.

Version B — OutputLag, eGamers: the early Chapter 1 spot is a balcony meditation with Lucia; some lists count 15 spots.

Our call: meditate at every spot the moment you see one and keep a Chapter 1 save. The count in this guide follows PSN's 18. Until the game confirms it, do not trust either location alone.
