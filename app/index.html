No mission replay, no New Game+

There is no way to replay a story mission and no New Game+. Make a manual save at the start of every chapter (plus one before the final mission). The game offers 15 manual slots plus autosave — use them. A chapter-start save is the only way back if you miss something.

Sources: PowerPyx, OutputLag, KeenGamer, eGamers — retrieved 2026-11-20.

Cheats disable trophies and saving

Cheats turn off trophies and saving on that save. The game has dozens of cheats, but they only unlock after the credits — and they must never be used on your trophy save. Keep one clean save for the whole platinum run.

Sources: PowerPyx, OutputLag — retrieved 2026-11-20.

Hidden trophies — spoiler mode ON · 28 masked until earned

28 of the 55 trophies are hidden on PSN until earned. With spoilers hidden they appear as masked bars, grouped by chapter — never as a wall of repeated cards.

Sources & honesty

Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation list (Exophase and PSNProfiles agree on all 55). Every guide in this getaway is written in our own words — no guide text is copied from PowerPyx, PlayStationTrophies.org, PSNProfiles, TrueTrophies or Exophase — and every trophy names its sources with a retrieved date. Five trophies (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin) have triggers not yet confirmed in public: they are labelled exactly that, and nothing about them is invented. Chapter count is an open question — the kit lists Chapter 1–4 plus a final mission, PowerPyx describes 5 chapters — so this guide does not claim a chapter total.
